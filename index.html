If I am LOW

  1. Sit down and check my sugar. If I cannot check, treat it as low.
  2. Take 15 g of fast sugar: half a cup of juice or regular soda, 3 to 4 glucose tablets, or 1 tablespoon of sugar or honey.
  3. Wait 15 minutes and check again. Still low? Take another 15 g.
  4. Once I am back up, eat a snack like crackers with peanut butter.

If I am HIGH

  1. Check my sugar so I know the number.
  2. Drink plain water.
  3. Take my medication only the way my doctor told me.
  4. Rest and check again in 1 to 2 hours.

Get help right away if

  • I am very confused, very sleepy, or cannot swallow
  • I pass out or have a seizure
  • I am still low after 2 to 3 rounds of treatment
  • I am vomiting, have stomach pain, or my breathing is fast

Go to the nearest hospital, or ask someone near me to get help right now. Never put food or drink in the mouth of someone who is unconscious.
